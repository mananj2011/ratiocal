# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Mistake 1: Over-explaining**
Don't try to show you know everything. Answer the question asked.

✅ **Instead:** Keep answers concise. If they want more detail, they'll ask.

---

❌ **Mistake 2: Getting defensive**
When someone questions your analysis, don't take it personally.

✅ **Instead:** "Great question! Let me show you..." (welcome skepticism)

---

❌ **Mistake 3: Using jargon without explanation**
Don't say "heteroskedastic volatility clustering" or "kurtosis"

✅ **Instead:** Use simple terms. "Downside risk" not "negative semi-deviation"

---

❌ **Mistake 4: Apologizing**
Don't say "This might be complicated" or "I'm not sure if this makes sense"

✅ **Instead:** State it confidently. "Our Sortino is 0.75 versus Nifty's 0.51."

---

❌ **Mistake 5: Reading from slides**
Don't face the screen and read

✅ **Instead:** Face the committee. Refer to slides, don't read them.

# EMERGENCY PHRASES (If You Get Stuck)

**If you forget a number:**
> "Let me refer to my analysis... [check cheat sheet] ...yes, our Expected Value is +27.7%"

**If you don't know an answer:**
> "That's an excellent question. Let me think through that... [pause] ...here's what I'd say..."

**If someone challenges your probability:**
> "I appreciate the skepticism. Even if we're 50% wrong - say 15% instead of 30% - our Expected Value is still positive because of our diversified scenarios."

**If you need a moment:**
> "Let me make sure I'm answering your question correctly... [pause, think]"

**Never fake an answer.** It's better to say:
> "I don't have that specific calculation handy, but the principle is..."
